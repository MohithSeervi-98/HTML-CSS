Mysuru Palace

Mysore Palace is a stunning historical royal residence located in the heart of Mysuru, Karnataka. Designed by English architect Henry Irwin, its architecture blends Hindu, Islamic, Rajput, and Gothic styles. Inside, it features the grand Durbar Hall, a stained-glass marriage pavilion, and a jewel-encrusted Golden Throne. The palace is world-famous for its spectacular illumination, lighting up with nearly 97,000 bulbs on Sundays and public holidays.
